How to paste it
  1. Type your name and job title in the boxes above. The preview updates as you type.
  2. Copy signature.
  3. Outlook: Settings › Accounts › Signatures (classic Outlook: File › Options › Mail › Signatures). Gmail: Settings › General › Signature.
  4. Delete the old signature and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Save, and send yourself a test email.

Your name and title are real text. Everything below them is an image hosted on outforceivan.github.io, so the card stays white in dark mode and every logo, badge and icon stays clickable. Readers may need to allow pictures.
