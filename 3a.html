How to paste it
  1. Copy signature above.
  2. Outlook: Settings › Accounts › Signatures (classic Outlook: File › Options › Mail › Signatures). Gmail: Settings › General › Signature.
  3. Delete the old signature, press Ctrl+V, save, and send yourself a test email.

Everything except your name and title is an image hosted on outforceivan.github.io, so those parts stay white in dark mode.
